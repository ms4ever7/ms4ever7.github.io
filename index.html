A DEX aggregator where the protocol math is written by hand.

Magical Creature

A rules-based crypto trading system, with its own research lab.

Private project. The code, parameters and results are not shared.
  1. Market dataDaily prices for a rotating universe
  2. SignalsTrend and breakout views per coin
  3. ForecastOne blended score per coin
  4. SizingVolatility targeted, with a no-trade band
  5. RebalanceOrders placed, report sent
Research labA separate backtester imports the production strategy module, and a shadow replay checks it against the real account day by day.
  • A scheduled job rebalances a rotating universe of liquid coins once a day, then sends a report to Telegram. The universe is rebuilt every month from market rankings and limited to what the exchange can actually trade.
  • Trend-following signals (a moving-average momentum view and a breakout view) are blended into one forecast per coin. Position sizes are targeted to volatility, and a no-trade band keeps small drifts from turning into fees.
  • A separate research repository holds the backtester. It imports the production strategy module instead of copying it, so a backtest runs exactly the code that trades.
  • A shadow replay rebuilds the real account day by day and compares what the bot did with what the model says it should have done.
